# Market Intelligence Agent – Indian Food Delivery

## Project Overview

The Market Intelligence Agent project focuses on analyzing the Indian food delivery market using Amazon Quick and a structured Food Delivery dataset.

The project combines dataset-based analysis with external market research to identify customer trends, competitive dynamics, market opportunities, potential risks, and actionable business insights.

The Market Intelligence Agent was developed in Amazon Quick to organize the available information, perform market research, analyze business patterns, evaluate the reliability of findings, and produce a final leadership-ready Market Intelligence Brief.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build a Market Intelligence Agent capable of analyzing a Food Delivery dataset together with external market research.

The project aims to:

- Identify important customer and ordering trends.
- Analyze food preferences and cuisine patterns.
- Examine delivery and location-related patterns.
- Understand competitive and market signals.
- Identify potential market opportunities and risks.
- Evaluate the reliability and confidence of the findings.
- Produce actionable insights for business decision-making.

## Dataset

A structured Food Delivery dataset was used as the primary internal data source for the project.

The dataset contains information related to food orders, restaurants, customers, locations, delivery performance, order values, cuisines, ratings, customer satisfaction, and other delivery-related attributes.

The dataset was analyzed through Amazon Quick to identify patterns and generate business-oriented market insights.

## Project Implementation

### 1. Dataset Preparation

The Food Delivery dataset was selected as the structured data source for the Market Intelligence Agent.

### 2. Amazon Quick Space

A dedicated Space was created in Amazon Quick to organize the project resources and provide the Market Intelligence Agent with access to the required information.

### 3. Dataset Upload

The Food Delivery dataset was uploaded to the Space so that it could be accessed and analyzed by the agent.

### 4. Market Intelligence Agent

The Market Intelligence Agent was created in Amazon Quick for analyzing the Food Delivery dataset and supporting market intelligence research.

### 5. Knowledge Source Configuration

The project Space was linked to the Market Intelligence Agent as its knowledge source.

### 6. Dataset Analysis

The Food Delivery dataset was analyzed to identify important patterns related to locations, food items, cuisines, order values, delivery distance, delivery delays, customer ratings, and customer satisfaction.

### 7. External Market Research

External market research was conducted to understand broader Indian food delivery market signals, including competitors, consumer ordering trends, pricing and discount strategies, delivery trends, regional dynamics, opportunities, and risks.

### 8. Market Analysis

The Food Delivery dataset and external market research were combined to develop a broader Market Analysis.

The analysis focused on:

- Competitive landscape
- Market trends and signals
- Customer and cuisine trends
- Regional insights
- Market opportunities
- Market risks
- Strategic implications

### 9. Reliability Evaluation

The major findings were evaluated based on their supporting evidence, source, assumptions, missing information, and confidence level.

The evaluation also identified important limitations and clarified what could and could not be concluded from the available evidence.

### 10. Final Market Intelligence Brief

The final output was a leadership-ready Market Intelligence Brief summarizing the key insights, opportunities, risks, confidence levels, limitations, strategic implications, and recommended next steps.

### 11. Final Project Space

All major project resources and outputs were organized within the Amazon Quick Space.

## Reliability and Limitations

The findings were evaluated based on the available evidence from the Food Delivery dataset and external market research.

The Food Delivery dataset provides useful structured information for analysis, but it should not automatically be treated as statistically representative of the entire Indian food delivery market.

Therefore, conclusions are limited to the information and locations represented in the dataset and should be interpreted alongside the external research.

The project also identifies data gaps and areas where additional real-world market data would be required for stronger conclusions.
